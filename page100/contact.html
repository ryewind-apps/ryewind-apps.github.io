About the news

PAGE100 shows headlines and short summaries from the public news feeds of BBC News and The Guardian. It doesn't write or change the news: every story names its source, and links to the full article on the publisher's website. Each source is credited in the app on page 096.

For a question about a story itself, please contact the publisher that wrote it.
