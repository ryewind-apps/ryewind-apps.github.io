Privacy policy

PAGE100 (the Android app and the Samsung TV app) is made by Ryewind. If you have any questions, email ryewind.apps@gmail.com.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app never sends anything about you to me.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • To show the news, weather and so on, the app asks the services listed below for them directly, as a web browser would.

What stays on your device

The app saves these on your phone, tablet or TV, and nowhere else:

  • your settings: the area you chose, the text size, and your tide times key if you entered one;
  • on Android, what each home screen widget shows (for example your team) and the pictures it last drew;
  • copies of pages it has fetched, so they appear quickly and still work offline.

Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling it, removes them all.

Your location

PAGE100 does not use GPS or ask for permission to know where you are. You choose your council area from a list, and the app uses the centre point of that area (not your own position) to ask for local weather, sea conditions, TV listings and regional news.

Services the app contacts

When you look at a page, the app fetches its content straight from the service that provides it. As with any website you visit, each service sees your device's internet (IP) address and basic details such as the app's name, and handles them under its own privacy policy. If you have PAGE100 widgets on an Android home screen, the app also fetches what they show in the background, about once an hour and when you tap a widget to refresh it, from the same services and in the same way. Phone numbers, email addresses and links

On a phone or tablet, tapping a phone number, email address or web link asks you first, then passes it to your phone's dialler, email app or browser. The app doesn't make calls or send anything itself, and what happens next is up to you and that app. "Send feedback" on page 090 starts an email with the app's version and your device's maker, model, Android version and software version written at the bottom, to help track down problems: you can see it, and delete it, before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Supporters' messages

If you buy me a coffee and leave a message, it is handled by Buy Me a Coffee under their privacy policy. I choose which messages to show on the supporters page (in the app and on this website) and show only a first name or nickname. If you'd like yours changed or taken down, email me and I'll do it.

Children

PAGE100 is not aimed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because the app doesn't send me any personal information, there is nothing about you for me to show, correct or delete, apart from any supporter's message (see above). For anything else, email ryewind.apps@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
