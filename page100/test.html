Thank you for helping test PAGE100!

Before a new app can go on Google Play, Google needs at least 12 people to test it for 14 days in a row. Joining takes about two minutes.

You need an Android phone or tablet (Android 7 or newer) and the Google account you use for the Play Store. Sorry, not iPhones.

Join in three steps

  1. 1 Join the testers' group. Open the link below, signed in with the Google account your Play Store uses, and tap Join group. Only I can see who's in it.
    Join the group
  2. 2 Become a tester. Open this link (with the same account) and tap Become a tester.
    Become a tester
  3. 3 Install it. On that page, tap download it on Google Play, then Install. If Play says it isn't available yet, wait ten minutes and try again: joining can take a little while to reach the Play Store.

Then

Set it up: choose your area, then tap any page number to go there. Swipe left and right to turn the pages. For widgets, touch and hold your home screen, tap Widgets, and find PAGE100.

Keep it for 14 days: please keep PAGE100 installed and stay in the test for two weeks, and open it now and then. If you leave early, Google stops counting you.

Tell me what you think: anything that looks wrong, is confusing, or you'd like to see. Use Send feedback on PAGE100's Play Store page, or email ryewind.apps@gmail.com.

Questions

Does it cost anything? No. PAGE100 is free, with no ads.

What do you learn about me? Only that your Google account joined the testers' group (I see its email address there). The app itself collects nothing: see the privacy policy.

"Item not found" or "not available"? Check the Play Store is using the same Google account you joined with (tap your picture in the Play Store to switch), and give it ten minutes after joining.

What happens after the test? You can keep PAGE100: when it goes public, your copy just updates. To stop testing, go back to the tester link and tap Leave the program, then uninstall.
