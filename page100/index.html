Page100

Remember flicking to page 100 for the news, the weather, the football scores? PAGE100 brings it back: the black screen, the blocky text, the coloured bands and the three-digit page numbers, with today's news and information.

Coming soon. PAGE100 is still being made and tested, and isn't in any app store yet.

Free. No ads. No accounts. No tracking.

What's on it (planned)

  • News: UK, world and where you are101
  • Sport: scores, tables and results300
  • Weather for your area400
  • Tides, seas and bathing water420
  • Featured articles and On this day500
  • Useful numbers and helplines180

Coming soon

Android phones and tablets: planned for Google Play. On a phone you tap the page numbers and swipe to turn the pages; no remote needed. Home screen widgets show your weather, the sea, your team or league, Formula 1 or the headlines, and keep themselves up to date.

Samsung smart TVs: being tested with a small group of viewers.
